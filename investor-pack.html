What BRIDGRAI is NOT

Not AGI. Architecture framework for systems that collaborate with humans, not replace them.
Not a cloud AI alternative. Does not compete with OpenAI/Anthropic/Google Cloud. Competes with the absence of private AI at edge.
Not a language model. Engine with structural constraints that can run any model under 3B parameters. The model is a guest, the framework is the house.
Not a universal solution. Optimized for: child safety, human intent notarization, local multi-agent consensus. Not optimized for: content generation at scale, enterprise support, or full-autonomy applications.
Not claiming superiority over RLHF/DPO. Different paradigms. RLHF optimizes for human preferences post-hoc. Resonance Engineering imposes structural constraints pre-hoc. They complement each other.
